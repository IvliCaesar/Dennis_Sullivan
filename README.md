# Dennis Sullivan — Geometric Topology

- `gtop.pdf`: D. Sullivan, *Geometric Topology: Localization, Periodicity and Galois Symmetry* (the 1970 MIT notes, ed. A. Ranicki, 2005).
- `sullivan-geometric-topology.tex` / `.pdf`: *Localization, periodicity and Galois symmetry: an account of Sullivan's geometric topology*. Localization and profinite completion of homotopy types and the arithmetic square; local and p-adic spherical fibrations; étale homotopy, the Galois action on Grassmannians and the Adams conjecture; real varieties and homotopy fixed points; the KO[1/2]-orientation of block bundles, G/PL at odd primes, and the Galois decomposition of topological bundle theory through the cocycle Θ; formal manifold categories.
